1. The one-page version

What we offer

You commit capital. We buy only homes that fit your written rules. The system checks every deal, reserves your capital, and reports back.

Who sells to us

People with a reason to move: probate, chain breaks, repossessions, relocations, short leases. Direct, not through a portal.

Why trust the numbers

We refuse to value a home without a real sale nearby. Every valuation is frozen. Every month we check it against real sales and show you the result.

What we are not

Not a fund that promises a return. Not for hands-off yield with no view of the risk. If that is you, we will say so.

2.2 How a deal moves

  1. Source. The scout finds a motivated seller.
  2. Appraise. The AVM values the home from sold comps. No comps, no number.
  3. Check. Pass or fail against your buy box, with the reason written down.
  4. Reserve. If it passes, the capital is ring-fenced for that deal.
  5. Offer. A written offer is drafted. A person approves it.
  6. Complete. Legal workflow, refurb, sale.
  7. Report. You see the outcome against the original forecast.

2.3 The human step

Automatic does not mean unattended.

  • Steps are automated: sourcing, valuing, checking, reserving, drafting, reporting.
  • Thoughts stay with a person: the vendor conversation and the final offer.
  • Vendor emails are never auto-sent. They are held for founder review.

In version 1, capital is committed automatically to deals that pass. The offer to the seller is approved by a person.

Decision for the founder: should any deal class skip approval later? Our recommendation: not until the AVM backtest has 200+ matched sales.

3.2 Honest valuation

Most automated valuation fails quietly. Ours is built to fail loudly.

  • It never values without a sale. If radius comps, postcode comps and postcode-sector sales are all empty, it stops. It does not fall back to an area average. This exists because of a real incident: a £750k semi was valued at £345k off a placeholder average.
  • It sizes the home from its own EPC certificate, matched by UPRN. On 65 leads this moved 7 valuations by over 5%.
  • It leans on price per square foot when it has enough sized comps. On 209 real sales (May to July 2026), median error fell from 15.6% to 12.3%.
  • It flags its own doubts. Wide ranges and contradictory comps go to a person before any offer.
What we do not claim. 12.3% is a hold-out result, not live performance. Our own target is under 8%. We are not there. See section 4.

3.3 Servicing: why it helps your return

  • 24-hour response.
  • We view every property.
  • Confirmed written offer within two working days of viewing, binding upon Kept for a week.
  • Completion in weeks, not months. As little as two weeks.
  • The price does not change, except in three stated cases.

Why you care:

  • Fewer fall-throughs. A seller who trusts the process does not walk. A fall-through costs you time and carrying cost.
  • Better terms. Sellers who need certainty accept a below-market price for it. That discount is your margin.
  • Repeat work. Probate solicitors and agents send more to people who treat their clients well.

We also say who we are wrong for. If a seller can clearly do better on the open market and has time, we tell them.

3.4 Visibility: you see what we see

This is the main point of difference.

  • A live view of your mandate: capital committed, reserved, deployed, returned. [TO BUILD]
  • Every decision has a written reason: passed, failed, or sent to review.
  • Every valuation is frozen. We cannot quietly change a number afterwards.
  • A monthly accuracy report: median error, bias, share within 10% and 20%, and whether the range caught the sale. Published even when it is bad.
  • Segment honesty: any segment under 100 sales is greyed out and marked "too few".
  • Deal updates through the existing update flow. Seller personal details never reach you.

Say what we can show. Do not say what we hope.

7. Risks, in plain words

  • You can lose money. Prices can fall. Refurbs overrun. Sales take longer.
  • The valuation can be wrong. Our hold-out error was 12.3%. On a £400k home that is about £49k.
  • Capital is illiquid. [State the exit terms]
  • Concentration. With few deals, one bad one hurts.
  • Key person. This business has one founder.
  • Regulation. Rules for pooled property investment can change.
  • Technology. Systems fail and data feeds go down. The no-sale rule means we stop, not guess, but stopping has a cost.

8. Who we are wrong for

  • Investors who want a guaranteed return.
  • Investors who need to withdraw at short notice.
  • Investors who do not want to read the risk section.
  • Investors who want us to buy at any price to hit a deployment target.

If that is you, we will say so.

9. Before this leaves the building

Do not send, post, or discuss this pack with any potential investor until every item is done.
  1. Legal review. Pooling investor money to buy property can be a collective investment scheme. Sending this pack can be a financial promotion. Only an FCA-authorised firm, or an exempt route, can issue one. We are not FCA authorised. Use a specialist solicitor on structure and on who can lawfully receive the pack. This document gives no legal view.
