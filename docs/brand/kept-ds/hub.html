The decisions · v2 · full document in the strategy file

We keep our word, so you can keep going.

Positioning: For UK homeowners who must sell — not want to — Kept is the cash buyer whose written figure is the completion figure, because we publish the maths, the method, and every promise we've kept. Warm on the phone. Immovable on the number.

The lines (decided)

  • Masterline: Our word. Kept.
  • Explainer: A price given is a price kept.
  • Agents: You keep your fee. Your client keeps their move.
  • Reserve: The most reassuring full stop in property.

Three governance tests

  • Widow test — readable aloud, gently, to a recent widow.
  • Ledger test — publishable next to the public record without wincing.
  • Full-stop test — needs an exclamation mark? Doesn't work.

The Welcome (the "welcoming" half)

One person by name, start to finish · the no-decision call · voice notes answered in kind · grief-literate copy rules (probate never gets "great news") · the Kept Kit on completion day.

Proof machine

The shown maths · The Kept Ledger ("31 signed, 31 completed at the signed price") · shareable trackers ("14 Marsh Lane. Kept.") · published methodology · refusal stats.

The mark — now as actual vectors

The dot is the logo.

Primary wordmark, reverse, monogram seal, and the dot alone — drawn as SVG so they scale and export. Set in the placeholder stack until the typeface decision (see What's still missing); the geometry and rules below survive any face.

Primary — ink on cream. The dot is drawn as a circle, not a period: it never inherits font rendering.

Reverse — bone on forest. The wax dot survives both grounds unchanged: it's the constant.

The seal — avatar, favicon large sizes, stamp, sticker, PDF header. Serif k: the seal is the one place the serif touches the mark (wax = document world).

The dot alone — 16px favicon, loading indicator, list bullets in brand decks. Smallest unit of the brand.

Does it survive small? The size ladder

kept. kept. kept. kept.

Rule: wordmark down to 18px; below that, switch to the seal; at 16px (favicon) the dot alone. Never letter-space the wordmark to fit — swap assets instead.

Clearspace & rules

Clearspace = one dot

Minimum space around the wordmark on all sides equals the dot's diameter. Easy to remember, impossible to argue with.

Never

  • ✕Uppercase KEPT — certainty doesn't shout
  • ✕Dot in any colour but wax (or bone on wax surfaces)
  • ✕Outlines, gradients, or shadows on the wordmark
  • ✕The dot omitted. Ever. It's the promise.

One voice from billboard to error message

Voice

Chain: kept.   Completion date: kept.   Onward purchase: kept.
Word given, 9:41am. Word kept, completion day.
Searches ordered.   Survey booked.   Done.

Always

  • Short declaratives ending in full stops
  • Name the trade-off out loud (below market, and why)
  • Say who we're wrong for, unprompted
  • First names. "I'm Sam." Never "our team".

Never

  • ✕Exclamation marks — anywhere, ever
  • ✕Urgency timers, "instant cash", countdowns
  • ✕"Great news" / "congratulations" in probate flows
  • ✕Claims faster than the live-site promise (binding rule in CLAUDE.md)

Probate completion message, verbatim standard: "It's done. The money is with your solicitor. We hope it brings some peace."

Honest ledger of what a complete identity still needs

What's still missing

1 · A committed typeface (the big one)

Everything so far uses system-font placeholders — fine for direction, not ownable. Candidates to trial in Claude Design: wordmark/UI grotesk — Söhne, Neue Haas Grotesk, or (open-source) Hanken Grotesk; serif — GT Sectra, or (open-source) Fraunces / Source Serif 4. Decision needed before any print or final logo export.

2 · The seal, professionally drawn

Today's seal is a gradient approximation. The real one wants an illustrator's pass: embossed wax texture, usable as print sticker, PDF stamp, and 180px avatar.

3 · Photography direction shot for real

The spec exists (overcast British light, thresholds, doorsteps, kettle on — no stock smiles). Needs one half-day shoot at the first completed properties, with vendor permission.

4 · Applications not yet designed

Offer-letter PDF template (the artefact vendors actually receive), transactional email set, the agent A5 desk card, WhatsApp Business profile, social avatar/headers, the Kept Kit envelope.

5 · The legal gate

Class-36 trademark search on "Kept" — still unrun, still ten minutes, still blocking everything public. Plus @wearekept handle checks on Instagram/LinkedIn/X.
