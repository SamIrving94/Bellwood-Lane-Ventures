# Freelance brief: PPC specialist, UK property lead generation

## Public brief (post this)

**Kept** buys London houses directly from sellers who need a fast,
certain sale: probate, broken chains, relocation. Small firm, two
founders, real cash, completion in as little as two weeks. We are
selective on purpose and say so publicly.

**The role.** Run our paid acquisition, hands-on. The strategy, landing
pages and copy exist — we need an executor, not an agency deck.

- **Channels:** Google Search (the core), Performance Max, Meta
  retargeting. Nothing else.
- **Budget:** £1,000/month media to start, scaling on results.
- **Landing pages:** live and converting-ready (/probate,
  /save-the-sale, /sell). You don't touch page copy.
- **Geography:** London postcodes we name; distress and probate
  intent keywords.

**How you'll be measured — one number above all:**
cost per qualified form submission. Under £250 we scale the segment;
over £600 we kill it. You report weekly: spend, submissions, CPL by
segment, and what you're changing next week. One page, no decks.

**Ground rules (non-negotiable):**

- Every ad text is approved by a founder before it runs. You own
  structure, targeting, bids and testing — never the wording.
- Banned in any ad: "instant cash", "we buy any house", countdown
  urgency, superlatives, and any promise faster than the website makes.
- UTM tagging on everything; conversion = form submission, tracked
  end to end before a pound is spent.

**Engagement.** 30-day paid sprint at a fixed fee, remote, UK hours.
Extension month-to-month on the numbers. Start: within 2 weeks.

**To apply:** a short note (no cover letter), two UK lead-gen accounts
you personally ran (property, legal, finance or home services ideal),
and the CPL story on each — where it started, where you got it, how.

## Internal: the 5 screening questions (score each 1–3)

1. "Walk me through a UK lead-gen account you ran: starting CPL,
   ending CPL, what moved it." (Specific numbers = 3. Vague = 1.)
2. "Our budget is £1k/month. Is that enough to learn anything?"
   (Honest answer: yes for search on high-intent terms, barely for
   anything else. Anyone promising volume at £1k = 1.)
3. "A campaign is at £700 per lead after 10 days. What do you do?"
   (Kill or restructure = 3. "Give it more time and budget" = 1.)
4. "We approve every ad text before it runs. Problem?"
   (Comfortable = 3. Pushes back hard = 1 — wrong fit for this brand.)
5. "What tracking do you need in place before spending?"
   (Asks about conversion events, UTM, form tracking = 3. "The pixel"
   and moves on = 1.)

**Red flags:** agency wanting a 3–6 month minimum; %-of-spend pricing
at this budget; "we handle the copy too"; case studies with traffic
numbers but no CPL; won't name the accounts they ran.

**Fee reality:** at £1k/month media, a good freelancer's fee will be
close to the spend itself. That's normal. Month one buys calibrated
CPL numbers, not volume.
